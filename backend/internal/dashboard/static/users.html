Users

Everyone has their own volumes and sees only those, and the screens never show an administrator anyone else's files. But an administrator can take over any account: by handing a volume to themselves, or by making a sign-in link for someone and registering their own passkey on it. Only make people administrators if you'd trust them with everyone's files. Both leave a trace the person can see (a lost volume, a passkey they didn't add) and are logged.

Invite links

Anyone with an invite link can make their own account, up to the number of uses, until it expires or you revoke it. They choose their own name and always get an ordinary account. An account made this way shows which link it came from, so a link that got shared too widely is easy to trace.
